**书法**

书法是以汉字为载体的传统艺术，依靠毛笔蘸墨书写，十分讲究笔画力度与篇章布局。

书法拥有悠久的历史，古代文人把书法作为必备修养，书信、匾额、诗词作品都会使用书法。

现在中小学开设书法课程，各类书法展览持续举办，帮助大众感受笔墨魅力。

书法沉淀千年人文底蕴，直观展现中国人的精神气质与古典美学。

Chinese calligraphy is a traditional art based on Chinese characters, written with brushes dipped in ink, and places great emphasis on stroke strength and overall layout.

Chinese calligraphy has a long history. Ancient scholars treated calligraphy as an essential accomplishment, using it in letters, inscribed plaques, and poetic works.

At present, primary and secondary schools offer calligraphy courses, and various calligraphy exhibitions are continuously held, helping the public experience the charm of brush and ink.

Chinese calligraphy carries thousands of years of humanistic heritage, directly showcasing the spiritual temperament of the Chinese people and classical aesthetics.
