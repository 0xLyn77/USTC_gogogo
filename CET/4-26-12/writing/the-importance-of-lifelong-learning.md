In the 21st century, because of the convenience brought by the Internet, it is easy for all of use to get knowledge and information. However, we are not the only ones who can use the Internet; our classmates and colleagues can use it too.

If you luckily join a company and stop learning new knowledge while your colleague keeps learning, you may gradually fall behind him. Eventually, he may get a promotion, and you may even find that he becomes your leader.

Some people may think, "Once I retire, learning will have nothing to do with me." However, this idea is totally wrong. Take a simple situation for example. If your gradchildren ask you, "What's this?" or "Why is it like that?", how embarrassing it will be if you cannot answer! What will they think of you?

Therefore, lifelong learning is important for all college students, and we should never give up our ability to learn.
