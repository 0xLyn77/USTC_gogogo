# stem

**以体育锻炼磨砺学生意志**

Suppose your university is planning to launch a program to help students improve their willpower through physical exercise.

Write an essay to offer practical suggestions on how to make the program effective.

You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

# answer

A man's willpower can be improved through exercises, whether it is physical or mental. So it is great to launch a program to help students improve their willpower through physical exercise.

But it's difficult to practice, since some students are not willing to change. So at the beginning of the program, we can set some gifts or something, give it to students who achieved some goals. Then when all of them get used to this program, we can devide them into like 3 groups by different shapes of their body. And give them different difficulty challenges.

Through different kind of students achieve different kinds of challenges, we can make everyone can take a lesson from this program, and solidly strengthen their willpower.

# refine

One's willpower can be improved through exercise, whether it's physical or mental. Therefore, it's worthwhile to launch a campus exercise program, and two practical steps can help make it effective.

However, it is difficult to implement, since some students are not willing to change. First, at the beginning of the program, we can offer small rewards and give them to students who achieve specific goals. Then, once most students get used to this program, we can divide them into three groups accourding to their fitness levels. We can then assign each group challenges of varying difficulty, helping them push past their limits step by step.

By allowing, students of different levels to complete suitable challenges, we can enable everyone to benefit from this program and steadily strengthen their willpower.
