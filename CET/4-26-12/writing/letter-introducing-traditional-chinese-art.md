During the Spring Festival, families gather to celebrate the end of a year's hard work and decorate their homes with red paper-cuts. Using only scissors or knives, people cut red paper into vivid patterns like flowers and animals. People have practiced this art for a long time; at first, they used paper-cuts to pray for safety. But at present, people always paste them on windows at the beginning of the Chinese Lunar New Year, which symbolizes driving away bad luck and welcoming good luck.
