You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

# refine

Dear School Committee,

I am writing to submit a proposal for our upcoming innovation and entrepreneurship competition, which aims to foster students' creativity and practical entrepreneurial skills. Open to all students across the campus, the event is proposed to last one full day.

The competition will consist of three main parts. First, we can invite several graduates who have succeeded in entrepreneurship to deliver speeches to kick off the event. Second, each participating team will present their innovative project. A panel of teachers will score them based on their originality and presentation performance. Finally, we can organize some interesting innovation-themed interactive games, followed by an award ceremony.

With these arrangements, we hope to make this competition both meaningful and successful. Thank you for considering this proposal, and i look forward to your valuable feedback.
